03 / Condition & fit

The label is a clue. The measurements are the answer.

Every garment is graded without dramatic language. Normal ageing is separated from actual flaws, then photographed and described.

Like newNo visible wear
ExcellentVery light signs of use
Very goodMinor, disclosed wear
Naturally wornVisible character, still wearable

Vintage sizing moves. Tape measurements do not.

We measure each piece flat and list the garment dimensions alongside the original label. Compare them with something you already own and like.

Cleaned, refreshed or left untouched—with a reason.

Cleaning status tells you whether a piece was professionally cleaned, gently washed, steamed, or left untreated because of fabric sensitivity.

We call out what could change your decision.

A softened cuff is normal ageing. A repaired seam or visible mark is a flaw. Both are described calmly and photographed where possible.

Size and measurement guide

Measure a garment you already own while it lies flat. Compare like for like rather than relying only on the size label.

Chest / bust

Measure straight across from underarm to underarm, then double it for the full circumference.

Waist

Measure across the narrowest point or the waistband, depending on the garment.

Length

Measure from the highest shoulder point or waistband down to the hem.

Vintage label note

A vintage size 14 can fit like a modern 10. The measured-fit note is our practical comparison, not a replacement for exact dimensions.

Condition guide

Condition changes the price, but honest wear can also be part of why a piece looks good. We separate normal ageing from flaws that may affect your decision.

Like new

No visible wear after close inspection. Shape, finish and hardware remain crisp.

Excellent

Very light signs of previous use, such as gentle fabric softening.

Very good

Minor wear is visible and disclosed, but the piece remains sound and ready to wear.

Naturally worn

Visible patina, fading or softened edges that suit the garment. Repairs and marks are still listed separately.

Flaw versus ageing

Fading on washed denim is ageing. An untreated stain or repaired tear is a flaw. We name both without making them sound alarming.

Cleaning labels

Professionally cleaned, gently washed, steamed, conditioned or untreated for material safety.
